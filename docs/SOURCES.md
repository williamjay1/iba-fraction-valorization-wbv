# Source provenance and reuse ledger

This release supports an attributed reanalysis of published model outputs and experimental tables. It contains 14 input files: 12 numerical caches/previously derived records, one public article XML and one cache-provenance manifest. The historical presentation package had 17 baseline files; its three unused access/download manifests are omitted here. The 38 derived CSVs are identified by the reference-results manifest. Input hashes are recorded in `validation/input_manifest.json`; existing source table, sample, worksheet and cell locators are preserved.

All eleven works supplying the numerical evidence have explicit CC BY 4.0 article notices or publisher licence metadata. Included supplementary records were checked for conflicting notices where the attachment was locally available; none was found. Retain source attribution and identify reformatting, linkage and calculation changes. See [DATA_LICENSES.md](../DATA_LICENSES.md) for the scope of the repository licences.

## Excluded works, software and disclosure

Song et al. (2026), [DOI 10.1016/j.cemconcomp.2026.106596](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cemconcomp.2026.106596), is an overlap comparator. Its [public author PDF](https://josbrouwers.com/publications/Journal313.pdf) was inspected; its first page reserves rights, including text/data mining and AI training. The complete PDF and its extracted full text are not distributed, and no numerical input is taken from it. Public access to that PDF is not treated as a redistribution licence. This source is distinct from the CC BY Spanish Marco-Gibert article.

Commercial background inventories, unavailable laboratory replicates and the original source article/supplement binaries are not bundled, except the explicitly CC BY Abis XML. The German inputs contain published outputs and selected foreground/source cells rather than proprietary ecoinvent process records. The included XML retains public author, affiliation and correspondence metadata; the numerical inputs contain material/sample keys and published study context, with no unpublished personal participant data or credentials identified.

The vendored [SciencePlots](https://github.com/garrettj403/SciencePlots) style files use their upstream MIT licence with the original copyright notice retained. K-Dense Scientific Agent Skills informed the visualization workflow as a referenced method; its full skill text is not included. Their developers are not identified as scientific authors of this work.

OpenAI Codex assisted discovery, retrieval, extraction, programming, checking, visualization, interpretation and drafting. The source measurements are attributed published records. This assistance is not experimental evidence or independent empirical validation. The human submitting authors must review the content and approve actual author, funding, contribution and competing-interest information. See [PUBLICATION_METADATA.md](PUBLICATION_METADATA.md). No repository DOI, research author or funding declaration is invented in this ledger.

## Verification scope

This licence check read article copyright/permissions sections, official publisher licence metadata, the included XML permissions element and the specified locally downloaded supplements. Official pages were accessed through the agent-reach reader; unavailable direct pages were checked with official publisher metadata or already downloaded published PDFs. Some direct ScienceDirect/MDPI requests returned 403/429; those failures are not reported as successful full-text retrievals. The new German official workbook was compared to every distributed source cell without recalculating the seven analysis modules. Attribution and permissions remain subject to the actual material-specific notices; this ledger does not guarantee every possible downstream reuse.
